About ToolsWebsite

ToolsWebsite is a growing collection of free online tools: finance calculators, PDF and image utilities and developer helpers. Every tool is built to do one job well and to open instantly, with no signup and no software to install.

Why your files stay private

Most tool websites upload your files to a server. Ours do the work inside your browser, so documents, images and numbers you enter never leave your device. That is the main reason this site exists.

How each tool is written

Every tool page explains what the tool does, shows how to use it step by step with screenshots, lists the formula or method behind the result, and answers common questions. Calculators give estimates for convenience; see our disclaimer.

How the site is funded

The tools are free. The site is supported by advertising, which is kept away from buttons and results so it does not get in the way.

Suggest a tool or report a mistake

If a result looks wrong or you want a tool we do not have yet, please contact us.
