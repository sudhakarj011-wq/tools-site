What is GST?

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is the single indirect tax charged on most goods and services in India. It replaced older taxes such as VAT, service tax and excise duty. Every business that sells above the registration threshold collects GST from customers and pays it to the government after claiming credit for the GST it paid on its own purchases.

Because GST is added on top of a price, it is easy to get the numbers wrong when preparing an invoice, comparing quotes or checking a bill. This calculator does the arithmetic for you and shows the tax split the way it appears on a GST invoice.

How to use this GST calculator

It takes three quick steps. Each one below shows exactly what you will see on the calculator above.

Step 1: Enter the amount and choose the calculation type

Type the amount in rupees. Then choose Add GST if your amount is the price before tax (for example, a quotation that says "plus GST"). Choose Remove GST if the amount already includes tax (for example, an MRP or a shop bill).

Step 2: Pick the GST rate

Tap 5%, 12%, 18% or 28%. Use the rate printed on your invoice or the rate that matches the item's HSN or SAC code. Most services, including IT and software services, are charged at 18%.

Step 3: Read the result

The total appears at the top, followed by the original amount, CGST, SGST and IGST. Use Copy result to paste the full breakup into an invoice draft, a spreadsheet or a message.

Example: removing GST from a bill

Say a bill totals ₹1,180 and you want to know the price before tax. Choose Remove GST, enter 1180 and pick 18%. The calculator shows the original price (₹1,000) and the GST contained in the bill (₹180).

GST formulas

Adding GST to a price

GST amount = Price × GST rate ÷ 100
Total = Price + GST amount

Removing GST from a total

Original price = Total × 100 ÷ (100 + GST rate)
GST amount = Total − Original price

CGST, SGST and IGST explained

When a seller and buyer are in the same state, the GST rate is split into two equal halves. At 18%, that means 9% CGST goes to the Centre and 9% SGST goes to the state. When the sale crosses a state border, the full 18% is charged as IGST instead. The total tax is the same either way; only the invoice breakup changes.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate GST on a price?

Multiply the price by the GST rate and divide by 100 to get the GST amount, then add it to the price. For example, 18% GST on ₹1,000 is ₹180, so the total is ₹1,180.

How do I remove GST from a GST-inclusive amount?

Divide the total by (100 + GST rate) and multiply by 100 to get the original price. For ₹1,180 at 18%, the original price is ₹1,000 and the GST is ₹180.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

For sales within one state, GST is split equally into CGST (Centre) and SGST (State). For sales between states, the full GST is charged as IGST.

Which GST rate applies to my product or service?

It depends on the HSN or SAC code of the item. Common slabs are 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%. Check the official GST rate finder or ask your CA to confirm the correct rate.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. This calculator runs entirely in your browser. The amounts you enter are never uploaded or stored.
